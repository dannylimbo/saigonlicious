# Saigonlicious – Product Context

## Register

**Brand** — marketing landing page for a local Asian takeaway & delivery restaurant. Design IS the product; visitors judge the business by this surface.

## Users & Purpose

- Residents of Lüneburg ordering Asian food for delivery or pickup
- Lunch-break customers looking for Mittagstisch
- Returning customers who need menu, hours, phone, and route fast

**Primary job:** get from landing to order (call, menu, route) in under 10 seconds on mobile.

## Brand Personality

**Urban · Fresh · Direct**

Warm street-food energy, not fine dining. Confident lime-green on charcoal, inspired by the physical menu boards.

## Anti-References

- Fine-dining white-tablecloth restaurant sites
- Generic SaaS cream landing pages
- Kitschy “Asian font” tropes
- Over-animated AI slop (gradient text, ghost cards, eyebrow on every section)

## Strategic Principles

1. Phone number and “Jetzt bestellen” are always one tap away on mobile.
2. Food photography carries appetite; UI stays dark and disciplined.
3. Brush-label category headers are a **named brand system** (from the printed menu), not generic section kickers.
4. Committed color strategy: charcoal base + saturated Saigon green accent (~30% of interactive surfaces).

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast on body text against charcoal
- Visible focus rings on all interactive elements
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected

## Tech

Next.js 15, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript. Content centralized in `src/lib/site-data.ts`.
